## Key distinctions

Permission must cover the activity, target, timing and third-party dependencies. A reachable system is not automatically in scope. Record evidence securely and agree retention and escalation arrangements before testing.

The original legal references below are study pointers, not current legal guidance. The syllabus still names the Data Protection Act 1998; distinguish that historical reference from current law. Consult current official legislation and organisational advice for actual engagements.

## Topic reference

* **Ethics & Professional Conduct**: CREST Code of Conduct, nondisclosure agreements (NDA), ethical boundaries.
* **Legislation & Regulations**:
  * UK Computer Misuse Act (1990) Sections 1, 2, 3, 3ZA, and 3A.
  * Data Protection Act / GDPR, Human Rights Act 1998, Police and Justice Act 2006.
* **Scoping & Rules of Engagement**:
  * In-scope vs. out-of-scope assets, testing windows, emergency escalation paths.
  * Third-party hosted environments (e.g., AWS, Azure shared responsibility models).
