## Historical content

The syllabus includes older technologies and laws. Study their mechanisms and weaknesses, but distinguish historical exam scope from present deployment advice. The original notes also include modern extensions such as SSRF and WPA3; inclusion does not establish an official exam weighting. For current legal requirements consult official legislation and appropriate advice.

This repository does not estimate question counts by group.
