No PDF was established to be an original repository-authored work. Attribution alone does not establish permission. The full commercial book must not be assumed to share the license of an online excerpt or tool manual.

## Existing-content policy

All existing PDFs, release assets and download URLs remain intact. No material was re-uploaded, replaced or removed. Owner approval and rights verification are required before changes to third-party distribution. Maintain a permission record identifying the rights holder, permitted use, scope and date.
